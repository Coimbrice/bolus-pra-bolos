Bolus pra bolos

A demo of a small TypeScript library that calculates insulin boluses with the method from Think Like a Pancreas. Everything runs in your browser.

Not a medical device. This is a programming exercise. It has not been clinically validated and must not be used to decide real insulin doses. Always follow the guidance of your diabetes care team.

How fast it digests

Fast: bread, rice, potatoes, cereal. Slow: low glycemic index foods, or long or very large meals.

Activity

Exercise in the 2 hours after the meal lowers the dose; long periods sitting still, like a flight, raise it.
